Privacy Policy

PerfectStack: Tower Pulse ASMR · Developer: Thinh Nguyen · Effective date: 2026-10-15

Summary

PerfectStack does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline and has no accounts.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your own progress and settings (for example best score, coins, unlocked items, appearance, sound and haptics preferences) locally on your iPhone using the system's standard UserDefaults storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer. Deleting the app removes it.

Permissions, tracking and third parties

The app requests no system permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos). It contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

In-App Purchases

Optional one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or personal information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

The current version does not use Game Center. If a future version adds it, it will be optional, only used when you choose to open it, and handled by Apple under Apple's terms. The game will remain playable without it.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
