How to play

Tap anywhere to drop the sliding block. Precise drops play musical notes. Choose Classic or Zen 100 (no collapse) from Home.

Restore purchases

If you bought Master Pass Lifetime or Instrument Pack and they are missing, open Settings (or the Pro screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you purchased with.

No sound?

The game respects the silent switch. Turn the switch off and check the volume and Sound settings in the app.
